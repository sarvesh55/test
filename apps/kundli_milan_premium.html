Accuracy & limitations

This standalone prototype is not a certified Panchang or professional ephemeris. It uses simplified astronomical formulae and an approximate ayanamsa. It does not yet calculate full Vimshottari Dasha, precise Navamsa, divisional charts, transits, or all traditional cancellation rules. For professional or commercial use, integrate a licensed Swiss Ephemeris implementation and validate the regional tradition, ayanamsa, house system and local birth-time conversion.

Astrology is a cultural belief system and is not scientifically validated as a predictor of relationship success. Use the report as a discussion aid, not as the sole basis for a life decision.
